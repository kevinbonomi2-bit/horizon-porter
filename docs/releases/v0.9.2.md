# horizon-porter 0.9.2

More of each mod's Java comes over, a port builds on whichever Horizon you have, and every port
now says in plain words what the translation left out.

**Re-port your mods with this version** (`port --force`, or Port again in the app), then
`translate --refresh`.

## Ports build on any recent Horizon

0.9.1 wrote the sound and particle calls for the Horizon it found on your computer. A port now
carries both forms and the compiler picks the one that fits, so the same port builds on an
instance that plays an older Horizon and on the newest nightly. horizon-porter's tests check both.

## What ported code can reach now

- **Sturdy faces** (`isFaceSturdy`, `canSupportCenter`, `canSupportRigidBlock`): what a block
  that rests on or hangs from another asks. From the block's shape where Horizon gives it.
- **The held item while placing** (`context.getItemInHand()`): a block that takes its colour or
  kind from its item.
- **Action-bar messages** (`displayClientMessage(text, true)`) and **breaking a block with its
  drops** (`level.destroyBlock`).
- **The mod's config, when it is a class of plain fields**: Cloth/AutoConfig, OmegaConfig,
  MidnightLib and the like, which most Fabric mods use. The values become the port's `Config`,
  kept in Horizon's mod settings with a page in Options > Mods. Beautify, Villagers Plus (49
  values) and Friends & Foes (86) get theirs.
- On Horizon's new mod API, a block placed in water knows it, and waterlogs as it does in Java.
- **Spawning entities** (`type.spawn`, `addFreshEntity`): from a click, an item, a block
  entity's tick and a block's own tick. Babies (`setAge(-24000)`) and thrown things
  (`shoot`, `setDeltaMovement`) too. An egg block hatches now.
- **Blocks that remove themselves** when a neighbour changes (`updateShape` returning air) and
  ones that schedule a tick there. The most common thing ports said they couldn't do.
- **Where the player stands, their eyes and the way they look** in a use; vanilla's **level
  events** (`levelEvent`), a block's **break effect** and its **own particles**
  (`BlockParticleOption`), **game events** for sculk, **sounds at a point**, whether a spot
  **sees the sky**, and the sky's and the blocks' light apart.

## Things you throw

A mod's thrown item (`ThrowableItemProjectile`: Farmer's Delight's rotten tomato, Naturalist's
duck egg) is now one of Horizon's thrown entity types, drawn as its item. 0.9.1 left every
projectile out. Its item's Java `use` is ported on top, so it flies the way the Java throws it,
with the mod's own sound. Golems and fish that the porter skipped as "not a mob" come over too.

Checked in game on regular Horizon: a rotten tomato thrown along the look, a pie popping off
when the block under it goes, and an ostrich egg cracking, hatching and leaving a baby ostrich.

## Five mods it had never seen

To check this isn't tuned to the mods above, five others went through the whole flow untouched:
port, translate with Claude Haiku, build, play.

| Mod | Functions ported | Cost | Blocks that place | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Animal Feeding Trough | 6 of 6 | $0.03 | 1 of 1 | plays |
| Enchanting Infuser | 7 of 7 | $0.05 | 2 of 2 | plays |
| Trading Post | 5 of 5 | $0.03 | 1 of 1 | plays |
| GraveStone | 16 of 16 | $0.13 | 1 of 1 | plays |
| Waystones | 68 of 68 | $0.79 | 31 of 31 | plays |

"Plays" means it builds, loads and its blocks place and stand. It does not mean the mod is whole:
what these five are for mostly sits where Horizon has no hook for ported code yet. A trading
post gathers the villagers around it, a grave is made when a player dies, a waystone opens its
own screen to teleport. Each port's TRANSLATION-NOTES.md says so function by function.

Two things they asked for are in this version: **a line in the player's chat**
(`sendSystemMessage`), and **the ids behind a mod's registry constants** (`ModSounds.EGG_HATCH`)
when that class is too long to show the translator.

## Refresh costs less

`translate --refresh` is selective now: a function ported before is redone only when its notes
name something this version gives it. Bringing Farmer's Delight up to date took 89 functions with
0.9.1; from 0.9.1 to this version it is a fraction of that.

## TRANSLATION-NOTES.md

After translating, the port folder has a `TRANSLATION-NOTES.md`: each ported function that
couldn't do part of what its Java does, and what that part is, in the translation's own words.

# horizon-porter 0.9.1

Fixes found by playing Farmer's Delight after 0.9.0, each made in the porter so every mod with
the same pattern gets it, and a way to bring an older port up to date.

**Re-port your mods with this version** (`port --force`, or Port again in the app), then
`translate --refresh`.

## Bring an older port up to date

- **`translate --refresh`.** A function ported by an older horizon-porter keeps its old port,
  even when a newer version would give it more to work with. `port --force` now marks the kept
  functions it describes differently and says how many could be ported better; `translate
  --refresh` ports those again from the Java, along with the ones whose port said something was
  out of reach. A function that fails keeps its old port. The app does it when you port again.

## What ported code can reach now

- **Tags, water and light.** A block's code can ask whether a block is in a tag, how much water a
  spot holds and how bright it is: what a plant's `canSurvive` and a crop's `randomTick` ask in
  Java. Where Horizon gives no tag query, the answer comes from a table of 26.3's block tags.
- **Dropped items, redstone and state in a tick**, on today's Horizon too: a basket or a hopper
  of the mod's own pulls in what lands at its opening, and stops when powered.
- **Experience.** A tick or a click can drop experience orbs.

## Content

- **Blocks that always hold water** (rice, a mod's seagrass: Java's `LiquidBlockContainer`) keep
  their water in Horizon.
- **Seeds of a class of the mod's own** (a `BlockItem` subclass) plant their crop.
- **`.mapColor(..)` and `.instabreak()`** carry over.
